WHO ARE WE

Welcome to the iOS Design Lab at Michigan State University. The lab is a one-of-a-kind university experience and is made up of students from various colleges and programs from within MSU. In the Lab, we work in diverse teams to discover issues within our communities and design impactful solutions using the Apple ecosystem. Currently, the iOS Design Lab is designed as a one-year cohort-based experience.

OUR MISSION

Discover

In the iOS Lab, we address the user from start to finish. In the discovery space, we assess our user’s needs and ask ourselves “How might we?”, when it comes to finding problems within their communities.

Create

Using a human-centered approach, we test and examine different technological solutions to each new space we work in to build and produce modern, intentional, and impactful solutions.

Impact

The communities we work with are the reason for our work, so we focus on building connections with our clients that keep them apart of the design process to result in something that actually works to fix the problems we set to solve.

OUR VALUES

Problem-Based Framework

We begin by discovering and understanding the heart of real-world problems.

Experiential Approach

We learn by doing and approach design through iterative and reflective practices.

Human-Centered Design

We design for humans first and seek to understand their needs, behaviors, and desires.

Value Creation for Users and Markets

We assess market spaces and design sustainable solutions for both users and industry.

Multidisciplinary Collaboration

We welcome individuals from a wide range of backgrounds and skillsets and provide space for diverse teamwork.
